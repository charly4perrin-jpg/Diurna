About

Diurna is a short daily briefing on world affairs, free and without advertising: five stories every morning, chosen by cross-checking the front pages of the major newsrooms, each explained in three sentences, with a globe to place them on.

Who makes Diurna

Diurna is a personal, non-professional project. Its publisher has chosen not to publish their identity, as French law allows in this case (see the legal notice).

How it is made

Stories are selected and written by an artificial intelligence model (Claude, developed by Anthropic), which follows a public editorial charter and cites its sources under every story. Nobody reviews the texts before they are published, which is why every page carries the line “Written by an AI from the sources cited”. The details are on the Method page.
