Privacy

Diurna sets no cookies, shows no advertising, asks for no account and sells no data. Here is, point by point, what happens when you read the site.

On your device

The site keeps two preferences in your browser’s local storage: the language you chose and whether the country profile is open or closed. To work offline, it also keeps a copy of its own pages. None of this is sent anywhere; you can clear it at any time by deleting the site’s data in your browser.

Audience measurement

No audience measurement is active at the moment.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub’s privacy statement.

Links to other sites

The sources cited under each story lead to other sites, which apply their own rules. Fonts, the map and flags are served by Diurna itself: no other external resource is loaded.

Your rights
